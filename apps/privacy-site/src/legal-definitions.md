## Definitions

These terms are used the same way in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.

- **The extension** — the BugCase browser extension you install in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
- **The dashboard** — the BugCase report viewer web page. It runs entirely in your browser; it has no accounts and no server.
- **Capture** — the act of generating a bug report from the current page, on your device.
- **Report** — the ZIP file a capture produces (its `report.json`, screenshots, and the self-contained `report.html`).
- **Device-local** — stored only in your browser or your Downloads folder on your own computer; never transmitted by BugCase.
- **The publisher** — the maintainer of the BugCase project, reachable through the public issue tracker at <https://github.com/bisensamiksha/BugCase>.
